# Q3 2026 Marketplace Fee Transparency Report

**Reporting Period: July 1 – September 30, 2026**

**Seller Transparency Hub**

---

## Executive Summary

This report describes documented marketplace fee structures and selected fee and policy developments relevant to the July–September 2026 reporting period for Amazon, Walmart Marketplace, eBay, Etsy, and Shopify.

It is a curated, non-exhaustive research record. It does not capture every fee or policy change that occurred, and it is not produced by real-time or continuous monitoring. Each material marketplace claim below is followed by a bracketed source reference (for example, [S1]) that links to the marketplace's own documentation; the full list appears under Sources.

Main observations:

- Seller costs on each covered marketplace consist of several components — percentage fees, fixed per-order or per-listing charges, subscriptions, fulfillment and storage fees — so a single headline percentage does not describe the cost of selling.
- Several documented developments fell in or near Q3 2026: Amazon's 3.5% fuel and logistics-related surcharge on fulfillment fees, which began April 17, 2026 and applied throughout Q3 [S6]; eBay Germany's selling-commission changes for business sellers effective July 1, 2026 [S12]; eBay's Shipping Service Standard Policy, effective August 31, 2026 for a defined set of cross-border sellers [S13]; and Etsy Regulatory Operating Fee changes for certain countries effective June 22, 2026 [S17].
- Many current fees apply only under specific conditions — category, price tier, plan, fulfillment program, season, or seller location — which makes direct comparison between marketplaces difficult.

---

# Scope and Method

**Marketplaces covered:** Amazon (U.S.), Walmart Marketplace (U.S.), eBay (U.S. standard schedule, plus the eBay Germany and cross-border developments named above), Etsy (U.S. fee basis unless stated), and Shopify (U.S. pricing).

**What was done:**

- Current fee statements were checked against the published Seller Transparency Hub Current Fee Database, whose records were reviewed against identified first-party marketplace pages in October 2026.
- Dated developments were checked against first-party marketplace announcements or policy pages. Announcements made through official marketplace community or seller-forum accounts are identified as such.
- Current values are kept separate from historical effective dates. A current fee page is not treated as evidence of when a fee changed.
- Tiered and conditional pricing is described explicitly rather than reduced to a single rate.

**Dataset state at time of review (October 29, 2026):**

| Dataset | Records |
|---|---|
| Current Fee Database | 38 current fee records |
| Historical Fee Changes | 3 documented historical events |
| Policy Archive | 1 documented policy event |

These datasets are curated and intentionally selective. Their size reflects the evidence standard applied, not the number of fee or policy changes that occurred across the marketplace economy.

# Marketplace Findings

## Amazon

### Observed data

- Referral fees vary by category and, for some categories, by total sales price. Examples: Home & Kitchen 15%; Toys and Games 15%; Consumer Electronics 8%; Electronics Accessories 15% on the portion of the price up to $100 and 8% on the portion above $100; Clothing and Accessories 5% at $15 or less, 10% above $15 up to $20, and 17% above $20. Most categories carry a $0.30 minimum per item [S1].
- Books carry a 15% referral fee plus a separate $1.80 closing fee per item [S1].
- The U.S. Professional selling plan costs $39.99 per month; Individual-plan sellers do not pay this subscription [S2].
- Base monthly storage for standard-size, non-dangerous goods is $0.78 per cubic foot from January to September and $2.40 per cubic foot from October to December; surcharges and program adjustments are additional [S3]. An aged-inventory surcharge and a conditional low-inventory-level fee may also apply [S4] [S5].
- Starting April 17, 2026, Amazon applied a 3.5% fuel and logistics-related surcharge to FBA fulfillment fees in the United States and Canada, calculated on fulfillment fees rather than on item sale price [S6]. It therefore applied during all of Q3.

### Post-Q3 development

Amazon announced that holiday peak fulfillment fees would apply from October 15, 2026 to January 14, 2027, with the 3.5% surcharge applied on top of peak fees [S7]. This falls outside Q3 and is recorded as an upcoming Q4 development.

### Analysis

An Amazon seller's fulfillment cost depends on a base rate that varies with product size and shipping weight, plus any applicable surcharge, seasonal storage rate, and inventory-level conditions. The report does not state a single "FBA fee," because no single value applies across products.

## Walmart Marketplace

### Observed data

- Referral fees are set by category. Examples: Consumer Electronics 8%; Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden 15%; Apparel & Accessories 5% at $15 or less, 10% above $15 up to $20, and 15% above $20 [S8].
- Some categories include exceptions. For example, Outdoors Products and Sports is 15%, except 8% for named subcategories such as binoculars and telescopes [S8].
- Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) charges a base fulfillment fee of $3.45 per unit for items with a calculated shipping weight of 1 lb or less; apparel, hazardous-materials, low-price and oversize charges can be additional [S9].
- WFS storage ranges from $0.75 to $7.50 per cubic foot per month depending on season and inventory age [S9].

### Analysis

Walmart's applicable cost depends on category, subcategory, total sales price, use of WFS, item weight, and storage period. Walmart's WFS terms are described here in Walmart's own terminology rather than by analogy to other programs.

## eBay

### Observed data (U.S. standard schedule)

- Most categories, including Home & Garden and Health & Beauty, carry a final value fee of 13.6% of the total sale up to $7,500 per item and 2.35% on the portion above $7,500 [S10].
- Books & Magazines carry 15.3% up to $7,500 and 2.35% above [S10].
- A per-order fee of $0.30 applies to orders of $10 or less and $0.40 to orders over $10 [S10].
- An international fee of 1.65% applies to applicable U.S. sellers when the delivery address or buyer's registered address is outside the U.S.; qualifying eBay International Shipping transactions are exempt [S10].
- Most sellers receive 250 zero-insertion-fee listings per month, then pay $0.35 per additional listing per category, subject to exclusions [S10].
- An optional Basic Store costs $21.95 per month with yearly renewal or $27.95 per month with monthly renewal [S11].

### Documented Q3 developments

- **eBay Germany (ebay.de):** From July 1, 2026, eBay Germany changed selling commissions for business sellers in selected categories, removing tiering in those categories, lowering commissions to 5% for listed used and refurbished item conditions, and revising the separate rate for sellers with a below-average service status [S12]. This change applies to ebay.de; it is not a change to the U.S. schedule above.
- **Shipping Service Standard Policy:** Effective August 31, 2026, eBay evaluates in-scope sellers on their Acceptance Scan Rate, the share of evaluated transactions with a valid carrier acceptance scan; the compliant standard is 100% [S13]. The policy covers only sellers with a registered address in the HiPO region, India, Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam, the Philippines, or Indonesia, for items shipped to the United States, United Kingdom, or Germany; local pickup and certain other transactions are excluded [S13]. Possible actions include holding payouts and adjusting selling limits [S13].

### Analysis

eBay combines percentage fees with fixed per-order charges and threshold-based marginal rates, and some developments are specific to one eBay site or seller region. A fee or policy change on one eBay site should not be read as applying to all eBay sellers.

## Etsy

### Observed data

- Listing fee: $0.20 USD per listing, whether or not the item sells [S14].
- Transaction fee: 6.5% of the listing price plus shipping and gift wrapping [S14].
- Payment processing for sellers with a U.S. bank account using Etsy Payments: 3% + $0.25 USD per order. Rates differ in other countries; this U.S. rate is not global [S15].
- Offsite Ads: 15% of an attributed order for sellers with under $10,000 in qualifying sales, and 12% for sellers who reach $10,000, capped at $100 USD per attributed order; enrollment rules apply [S16].
- Regulatory Operating Fees: Etsy announced changes effective June 22, 2026 for sellers in certain countries, including France (0.47% to 1.14%), Italy (0.32% to 0.80%), the United Kingdom (0.32% to 0.48%), and a new 1.97% fee in Hungary [S17]. Etsy's help page lists the resulting rates by country [S18]. These fees do not apply to U.S. sellers under the published list [S18], and they continued to apply during Q3.

### Analysis

An Etsy sale can incur a listing fee, a transaction fee, a payment-processing fee, and potentially an advertising fee and a country-specific regulatory fee. The total depends on seller location, sales volume, and advertising participation.

## Shopify

### Observed data

- The Basic plan costs $39 USD per month billed monthly, or the equivalent of $29 USD per month billed annually [S19].
- U.S. Shopify Payments online card rates differ by plan: 2.9% + $0.30 on Basic, 2.7% + $0.30 on Grow, and 2.5% + $0.30 on Advanced for standard online cards [S19].

### Analysis

Shopify costs separate a subscription from payment processing, and processing rates depend on plan. Rates for third-party payment providers and non-U.S. pricing are not covered in this report.

# Cross-Marketplace Observations

- **Fees are multi-component.** Every covered marketplace combines at least two kinds of charges, such as a percentage fee with a fixed per-order fee (eBay), a listing fee (Etsy), or a subscription (Amazon Professional, eBay Store, Shopify).
- **Thresholds work differently.** Some marketplaces apply one rate to the whole price based on a tier (Amazon and Walmart apparel), while others apply different rates to portions of the price (Amazon Electronics Accessories, eBay above $7,500).
- **Scope matters.** Several Q3 developments applied only to a specific site, seller region, or country (eBay Germany, eBay's cross-border shipping standard, Etsy's country-specific fees).
- **Operational policies affect sellers too.** Requirements such as eBay's Acceptance Scan Rate standard can lead to account actions without being a fee.

These observations describe structure. This report does not establish whether total seller costs rose or fell across the industry in Q3.

---

# Limitations

- **Non-exhaustive coverage.** The datasets and this report cover selected fees, categories, and events. Absence from this report does not mean a fee or change does not exist.
- **Documentation changes.** Marketplace pages can be revised after review; values may differ when read later.
- **Historical evidence can disappear.** Announcements may be removed or moved, which limits what can be verified later.
- **Current pages do not prove past timing.** A current fee schedule does not establish when a fee took effect, so effective dates are stated only where dated evidence exists.
- **Applicability varies.** Fees depend on seller type, category, product, plan, fulfillment method, and geography. U.S. figures should not be applied to other countries.
- **Fulfillment and storage need more inputs.** Size, weight, season, inventory age, and program affect these fees and are only partly represented.
- **Terminology differs.** Marketplaces use different names for similar charges (referral fee, final value fee, transaction fee), and categories are not defined the same way across platforms.
